# Skylit Academy playbook (Chris KB overlay)

Use this whenever you read Heatseeker/Flowseeker, grade a setup, or propose an ES/NQ/GC/SL plan. This is Glitch/Skylit doctrine as it lives in Chris's `Skylit/Knowledge-Base` (HSMapReviewSKILL, agent reference, pattern cards, Futures Clinic, Patternpedia). Heatseeker is context, never a signal generator.

**Conflict rule:** Chris's knowledge base wins over the public Academy pages. Name the conflict when it matters. Never invent a node. Cite `asOf`, `spot`, and metric on every heatmap read.

## Charts first, futures where

Heatseeker tells **why**. Futures tell **where**.

1. Draw structure on **ES/NQ** (cleaner overnight highs/lows, no cash-session gaps). SPX/SPY/QQQ are for gamma only.
2. Form a specific thesis from that structure (hourly levels first). Do not stare at 3-minute unless price is at a key hourly level.
3. Convert the Heatseeker node (SPX/SPY/QQQ) onto the futures chart. ES inherits SPX (±5 pts). NQ inherits QQQ (±$0.50 scaled by live NQ/QQQ).
4. Confirm or kill with Heatseeker (Trinity + VEX). Execute on ES/NQ at the converted tap, not mid-node.

VIX is a charted instrument. VIX into resistance favors ES longs. VIX consolidation break up = bearish trend day; break down = bullish. When VIX is expanding, **VEX overrides GEX**.

Sessions (ET): Asia 7pm-2am, London 2am-8am, NY 8:30am-4pm. Mark London high/low at 8am as liquidity-hunt magnets. Timmy Hunt: wait for the wick through the level, enter the rejection back through. SMT: NQ/ES diverge at the extreme, treat as exhaustion.

## Ten Commandments

1. Protect capital first.
2. Reversals only at floors or ceilings. No diddling in the middle.
3. Asymmetric R:R only (minimum 3:1).
4. Always know where price was **delivered from**.
5. Broader market / Trinity awareness.
6. Technical analysis before Heatseeker.
7. Confluence between nodes and price action.
8. Do not oversize. Scale with conviction.
9. **Long on red candles, short on green candles. Never chase.**
10. **Do not let a green trade go red.**

## Vocabulary

- **Pika** (yellow): +GEX. Friction. Buy dips, sell rips.
- **Barney** (purple): -GEX. Fuel. Pro-cyclical.
- **Absolute Value Rule:** size first, sign second. Biggest `abs(value)` wins.
- **King:** largest absolute node. One per session. Magnetic into the cash close. Can be Pika or Barney.
- **Floor / ceiling:** largest node below / above spot, not the nearest.
- **Gatekeeper:** checkpoint between spot and a larger node.
- **Air pocket:** empty stretch. Pathway, never a fade target.
- **Clear Skies:** no overhead nodes. Full-send continuation / reversal, not a gap to invent.
- **Empty Basement:** no nodes under a floor. Hard floor even if SPX shows a lower magnet.
- Zones, not ticks. SPX/ES ±5 to ±10 around a high-value node. Never a cent-precise reversal.

## Session map (under two minutes)

1. King.
2. Strongest floor and ceiling.
3. Gatekeepers on the path.
4. Air pockets.
5. Where is spot: floor, ceiling, Gatekeeper, or air?
6. Trinity: SPX, SPY, QQQ. Metals: GLD/SLV.
7. VEX + VIX: who is in control?

Never trade the midpoint.

## Live map (hard rule)

**Never trade, ping, or grade a frozen or stale Heatseeker map.** It reshuffles fast enough that the next refresh can invalidate the take.

- Live copilot: fresh Heatseeker every cycle (5 min). Cite that cycle's `asOf`. If king/floor/ceiling polarity or the working node is gone vs last cycle, the setup is dead - do not hold the old thesis.
- Backtests: a fill is legal only on the map whose `asOf` is the latest at-or-before the fill bar. If the next snapshot drops that node, flips polarity, or moves the king to the other side, treat it as scratched (refresh invalidation), not a runner.
- Do not replay a whole day off the 9:35 print. That is how the 8/10-21 majors-only tape went quiet, and it is not how Talon or this copilot trades.
- A+ still needs chart + live Heatseeker + execution. A correct 9:35 node that is gone by 10:05 is not A+.

## Regime

- **+GEX, VIX calm:** fade extremes. Wider stops, tighter targets (next node).
- **-GEX trend:** follow. Tighter stops, wider targets.
- **VEX / Rainbow Road:** VEX magnitude >> GEX, VIX expanding. GEX levels are unreliable. Trade **with** the trend. Target distant VEX nodes that come back to life. Do **not** sit this out just because Patternpedia also uses "Rainbow Road" for a structureless map.
- **Rainbow Road (Patternpedia):** no coherent node structure at all. Sit. Different meaning. Label which one you mean.
- **Whipsaw:** index conflict or equal opposing nodes. Fade extremes with limits. No directional chase.

## Patterns (magnitude still overrides)

- **Rug:** +GEX stacked above -GEX, no floor. Short the yellow rejection into green. If purple grows on the break, it's real. If purple shrinks, liquidity hunt / fakeout. Cover at next node or ride the air pocket.
- **Reverse Rug:** -GEX above +GEX. Long the yellow floor after purple rejects. Scale 30% first Gatekeeper, 40% King, 30% overshoot. Clear Skies above = slingshot.
- **Gatekeeper fade:** fade tests 1-2 of a yellow node (~66% reject). **3rd test flips to ~66% breakthrough. Do not fade the 3rd.** If the market opens above the King, high odds of a fade back through it.
- **Whipsaw:** two opposing nodes within ~10-15% magnitude. Limits only, lowball $0.10-$0.20. Long red into floor, short green into ceiling. If one node grows and the other shrinks, stop fading, follow.
- **Beach Ball (this KB):** price into a major **-GEX pit**. ITM-put unwind trampoline. Limit buy on red into the pit. Never chase. 3:1 required. Pit velocity +20% = magnet. 3rd touch of the pit favors break, not bounce. (Public Academy also uses "Beach Ball" for a +GEX overshoot snapback. Call that **overshoot snapback**. Do not chase punch-throughs as breakouts.)
- **Floor Bounce / Empty Basement:** hard floor, nothing underneath. 2-of-3 Trinity is enough; if QQQ holds, SPX's lower magnet can be overruled. Scale 25-50% starter at ±5, full size on the deeper dip. After bounce the floor node should **shrink**. If it **grows**, fake bounce (bearish divergence).
- **Trend:** King far from spot, void on one side. Directional only. No mean reversion. Do not chase the 3:30-4:00 ET unwind.
- **Pika Cloud:** dense +GEX cluster. Chop. Size down on anything that has to punch through.
- **Stairstep:** fresh node delivers into the next growing untested node.

## Node lifecycle

Fresh ? tested ? delivered ? decaying.

King / major-node **reaction** (Academy): 1st ~80% full size, 2nd ~66% size down, 3rd ~33% small or pass, 4th spent.

Gatekeeper **fade vs break** (this KB, wins for intermediate yellows): 1st/2nd fade, 3rd do not fade.

**Sloppy Seconds:** after a tap, if node value drops, the level is dead. Do not re-trade it.

Real nodes grow across sessions. Hedge/decoy nodes are far OTM, fade, look huge. Ignore bleeders. Far-OTM >3-4% from spot is dormant unless VEX regime is on. Label nodes Active / Warming / Dormant. Multiple Gatekeepers between spot and a far target kill the target.

V-shape fakeout: break of a node **and** the node value increases = hunt, fade the break.

## Execution

Enter only at a direct node tap, inside the deflection band:

- SPY / QQQ: ± $0.50
- SPX / ES: ± 5 points
- NQ: QQQ band scaled by live NQ/QQQ

Long red, short green. Limits. Never market-chase.

Stops: one node beyond invalidation. Minimum 3:1 or pass.

+GEX: fade, wider stop, tighter target.
-GEX: follow, tighter stop, wider target.

Futures cannot size for zero. Be pickier than options. Prop (Topstep): no news window 5 min either side of CPI/NFP if the firm forbids it; consistency so best day stays under ~$1,500 of the $3,000 target; default 5 MES / 3 MNQ (~$375 risk) until MLL buffer exists.

Do not average down unless the add was planned pre-trade (scale-in) and Heatseeker still confirms. Unplanned averaging is a hard pass.

FOMO pass: if price has already traveled more than 60% of the expected 3R, do not chase the tap. It is gone.

## A+ (all three)

1. ES/NQ chart structure names the location (hourly, then 3m trigger).
2. Heatseeker: **live** high-magnitude node at that converted level (the asOf at the tap, not the open print). King or empty-basement floor beats a thin twice-tapped Gatekeeper.
3. Tap entry, correct stop, ?3:1, regime-matched, commandment 9-10.

Missing one = B or pass. Full Trinity = full size. 2 of 3 = size down. Divergence = wait. SPX as the outlier is more serious than QQQ lagging, except Empty Basement: QQQ/SPY floor can overrule an SPX lower magnet.

## Hard passes

No chart thesis. Midpoints. Sloppy Seconds. Fading a 3rd Gatekeeper test. Fading inside an air pocket. Chasing overshoots as breakouts. Directional trades on a structureless map. Trinity divergence (unless Empty Basement exception). No 3:1. Fighting a reshuffled map. Letting a winner turn into a loser. 3:30-4:00 chase. Unplanned average-down. 60% of the R already gone. Kill-switch lockout. F map / ambiguous map. Opposition inside 3R. Third test of the same node this week. News/black-swan window. VIX 15-20% gap until the map settles. **Any setup graded off a frozen or stale map.**

## How to speak a read

Chart thesis (ES/NQ) ? converted node ? King / floor / ceiling ? GEX vs VEX control ? Trinity ? tap count ? A+ or pass. Cite `asOf`. Do not dump every strike. Never present a Heatseeker print as an order.
