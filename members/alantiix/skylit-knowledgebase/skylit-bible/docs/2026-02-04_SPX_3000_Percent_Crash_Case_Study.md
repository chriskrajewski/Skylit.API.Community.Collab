## 1. Concrete Setups & Patterns

### The "Rug Pull" Setup (SPY/SPX)

- **Configuration:** A positive GEX (yellow) node stacked directly on top of a negative Barney (purple) node [13:04, 22:04].
- **Logic:** The positive node acts as a temporary ceiling, while the underlying negative node is "pro-cyclical"—if price breaks below it, dealer selling accelerates to hedge, creating a flush.

### The King Node Overshoot (SPY 695)

- **Setup:** Price opened above the major King Node at 695 [08:49].
- **Logic:** Markets rarely pin a King Node for 9 hours straight. An open above the King Node suggests a high probability of a "fade" back through it [07:05].

### The "Air Pocket" / Void Zone

- **Setup:** QQQ had no GEX support nodes visible between 630 and 622 [13:15].
- **Logic:** A lack of nodes means there is no dealer hedging "wall" to catch price. This indicates "free-fall mode" or a "liquidity void" [33:04].

### The 6855 "Hedge Node" Snipe

- **Setup:** SPX had a massive, growing negative node at 6855 that had been present for days [19:14].
- **Entry:** Used a ±5 point margin of error for SPX. Limit buys for calls were set at 6860 as price approached the 6855 node [25:04].

## 2. New or Refined Concepts

- **VEX Taking Control:** GEX usually controls the 0DTE intraday movement, but if the VIX spikes, VEX (Vanna Exposure) takes over. In this state, distant VEX nodes (like the 6855 node) become highly magnetic [02:55, 03:43].

- **Node Delta Decay / "Coming Back to Life":** If price moves away from a node, the exposure numbers decrease (delta decay). However, the position is still there. If price returns to that level, the node "comes back to life" aggressively as dealer hedging obligations re-expand [17:51, 31:04].

- **Pro-cyclicality of Purple Nodes:** Negative GEX (Purple/Barney) nodes are pro-cyclical: buying leads to more dealer buying, and selling leads to more dealer selling. Breaking below them triggers a "sell loop" [12:32].

- **The Trinity Pre-Flight Check:** Simultaneously check SPX, SPY, and QQQ. On this day, SPY was at a King Node ceiling, and QQQ was in a downward void, which provided the confluence to ignore any "bullish" bias [04:05, 07:53].

## 4. Rules & Edge Cases

### Node Overshoot Margins

- **SPY/QQQ:** Allow for ±$0.50 overshoot [33:41].
- **SPX:** Allow for ±5.00 point overshoot [15:22, 33:44].

### The 3rd/4th Test Rule

Nodes generally hold for the 1st or 2nd touch. By the 3rd or 4th attempt, the node is "suspect" and highly likely to flush [16:55, 32:04].

### News as a Catalyst

Large "Hedge Nodes" (like 6855) often precede news events. Dealers/insiders leave "clues in the stars" by positioning at extreme levels days in advance [19:26].
