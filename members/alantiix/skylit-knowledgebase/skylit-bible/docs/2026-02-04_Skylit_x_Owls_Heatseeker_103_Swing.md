## 1. Concrete Setups & Patterns

### The "Stair Step Up and to the Right" (Swing Bull)

- **Setup:** Identify a ticker where the "King Node" (largest magnet) shifts higher week-over-week.
- **Example (LULU):**
  - Current Week: Floor at 157.5, upside to 165.
  - Next Week: Floor at 165, upside to 170.
  - Week 3: Upside to 175.
- **Logic:** This "unidirectional" flow confirms dealers are positioned for a sustained grind higher over several weeks.
- **Execution:** Buy the dip into the current week's floor (157.5) to ride the elevator up to the future targets (175) [07:53].

### The "Asymmetric Risk/Reward" Filter

- **Criteria:** For swings, the potential upside must be at least 7:1.
- **Calculation:** If downside risk is $2.50 (to the floor) and upside target is $15.00 (to the distant King Node), the trade is valid.
- **Rejection:** Do not take trades where the upside is only 3-5 points on a $150 stock; theta will destroy the contract before the move completes [07:08].

### The "V-Shape" Reversal (SPX Example)

- **Setup:** SPX dips into a major GEX floor (6630) that is significantly smaller than the node above it (6650).
- **Logic:** The "Gatekeeper" (6630) holds, and because the magnet above (6650) is 2/3rds larger, price snaps back violently to the larger magnet.
- **Outcome:** 40-point pump with almost zero red candles [19:18].

## 2. New or Refined Concepts

### GEX vs. VEX Roles

| Metric | Use Case | Timeframe |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **GEX (Gamma Exposure)** | Short-Term Precision | 0DTE/Weekly — defines immediate floor and ceiling for entry/exit |
| **VEX (Volatility Exposure)** | Long-Term Forecasting | 2-4 Weeks — reveals "hidden" targets dealers are hedging for next month |

- **Workflow:** Use VEX to find the direction (Bull/Bear), then use GEX to find the entry (Dip into Floor) [03:17].

### Low Liquidity Warnings

- **Rule:** If a ticker has GEX nodes with < 4 digits (e.g., thousands instead of millions), avoid it.
- **Risk:** Low liquidity tickers (like Wingstop or ISRG) are unreliable because a single whale can invalidate the map. Only trade tickers with "millions" in exposure [32:40].

### Implied Volatility (IV) Trap

- **Concept:** Do not buy pullbacks on tickers that just ripped (e.g., CoreWeave/Tesla). The IV is "juiced," meaning options are expensive.
- **Strategy:** Buy tickers that are "boring" and consolidating (Low IV) before the rip. Be a "Sleeper," not a "Chaser" [48:21].

## 3. Operational Workflow Items

### Scanning Routine (Nightly)

- **Workflow:** Spend 10-15 minutes every night scrolling through 300+ tickers using the arrow keys.
- **Goal:** Look for the specific "Stair Step" pattern. Ignore everything else.
- **Short-Cut:** Use `Command+G` (GEX) and `Command+V` (VEX) to instantly toggle views while scrolling [51:35].

### The "First 30 Minutes" Rule

- **Routine:** Glitch does not prepare extensively pre-market. He wakes up, scans the map for 15-30 minutes after the open (9:30-10:00 AM) to see where real money is positioning, then trades [59:14].

### Debit Spreads for Swings

- **Usage:** If trading a high-IV ticker or a swing (3-4 weeks out), use Call Debit Spreads to reduce cost basis and neutralize theta decay.
- **Contra-Indicator:** Do not use spreads on VIX or cheap tickers where capping gains limits a potential 1000% runner (like VIX to 100) [01:09:04].

## 4. Rules & Edge Cases

### The "3-Digit" Filter

- **Rule:** Ignore any GEX node that is not at least 4 digits (e.g., 1000+). 3-digit nodes (e.g., 500) are noise and should not be treated as hard walls [32:40].

### NDX (Nasdaq 100) Ban

- **Warning:** Do not trade NDX options. The bid/ask spread is massive ($1400-$1800), and liquidity is so thin that you can get trapped in a winning position unable to sell. Trade QQQ instead [01:11:42].

### The "Relative Strength" Divergence

- **Trap:** If a ticker looks good on Heatseeker (e.g., QCOM target 175) but the Price Action shows no structure/support (just a straight rip), skip it.
- **Rule:** Heatseeker must align with Market Structure (Double Bottoms, Breakout Retests). If PA doesn't support the map, PA wins [53:17].
