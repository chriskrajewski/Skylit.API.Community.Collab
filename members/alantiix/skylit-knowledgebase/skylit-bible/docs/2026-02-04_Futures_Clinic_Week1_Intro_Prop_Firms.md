# Futures Clinic Week 1: Introduction to Futures & Prop Firms

**Source:** [YouTube - Futures Clinic Week 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pBw_7cBkkQ)

This analysis synthesizes Futures Clinic Week 1 by John Wicks. While "Week 2" focused on technical analysis, this session establishes the structural framework for trading Futures (ES/NQ) using Prop Firms and bridging the gap with Heatseeker/Skylit methodology.

---

## 1. Concrete Setups & Patterns

### Liquidity Hunts (Search & Destroy)

- **Setup:** Price sweeping key session highs/lows (London Highs, Asia Lows).
- **Logic:** "London Highs" or "Asia Lows" act as liquidity magnets. Institutions hunt these levels to grab liquidity before reversing.
- **Execution:** Watch for a tap/sweep of these specific session levels followed by a reversal back into the range [13:20].

### The "Heatseeker Bridge"

- **Concept:** "Heatseeker tells us WHY; Futures tells us WHERE."
- **Workflow:** Use Heatseeker to identify the structural pressure (GEX walls/magnets) on SPX/SPY/QQQ, but execute the trade on ES/NQ at a precise futures level [03:34].

## 3. Operational Workflow Items

### Price Converters (Critical Tool)

- **Tool:** Use specific TradingView indicators (Author: PT Gambler or OEX Trades) to overlay QQQ prices on NQ charts and SPY prices on ES charts.
- **Function:** This allows you to see Heatseeker GEX levels (e.g., QQQ 623) directly on your futures chart in real-time [51:01].

### Prop Firm Selection Hierarchy

1. **Tradeify** — Most trader-friendly rules (recommended)
2. **MyFundedFutures**
3. **Take Profit Trader**
4. **Topstep**
5. **Apex** — Best for multi-account scaling (20 accounts), but stricter rules/denials [41:10]

### The "Sim-Funded" Buffer

- **Strategy:** When starting a funded account (e.g., $50k), size down (micros only) until you build a buffer above the trailing drawdown (e.g., +$2,000 to reach $52k). Only then size up to minis [36:33].

## 4. Rules & Edge Cases

### News Trading Restriction

- **Rule:** Many prop firms (like Tradeify) prohibit opening trades 5 minutes before/after major news (CPI, NFP). Breaking this leads to payout denial [30:18].

### Consistency Rule (30% Rule)

- **Rule:** No single day can account for more than 30% of your total profit at withdrawal time.
- **Implication:** You cannot "YOLO" one massive trade to pass. You must show consistent, repeatable gains [31:13].

### Sizing for Zero (The Futures Difference)

- **Contrast:** In options, you can buy a $50 call and "size for zero" (lose it all). In futures, you cannot size for zero because losses are linear and can exceed account balance (liquidation).
- **Adaptation:** You must be "pickier" with entries and use tight stop losses (e.g., 10 points on NQ) [37:52].
