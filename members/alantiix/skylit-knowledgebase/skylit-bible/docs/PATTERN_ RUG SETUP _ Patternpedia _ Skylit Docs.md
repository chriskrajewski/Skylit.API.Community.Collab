1PATTERN: RUG SETUP
by John Wicks
When we have a yellow node stacked above a purple node with no obvious
floor in sight, we can identify the setup as a rug setup.
•Why? - The negative gamma ceiling will accelerate the rejection of the
positive gamma ceiling, almost like "setting gasoline on the fire". The
negative gamma floors at 670 and 669 will also accelerate the move
down.

can get rapid and violent reactions to the downside.
▪All those negative gamma nodes below would also accelerate
price nosediving, with no clear floor in sight.
