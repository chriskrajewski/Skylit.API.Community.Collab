## 1. Concrete Setups & Patterns

### The "Price Delivery" Reversal

- **Setup:** Price drops from 6450 to 6405 (near a 6400 King Node) and bounces.
- **Error:** A trader wakes up, sees the 6400 King Node, and shorts at 6450 expecting a retest.
- **Correction:** If price has already tested the zone (6405) and bounced, the move is done. Do not short the bounce unless a new King Node forms above price at 6450 [01:25].

### The "Clear Skies" Reversal

- **Setup:** Price hits a major floor (King Node) and reverses, but the Heatseeker map shows no upside nodes.
- **Logic:** Users hesitate because they see no magnet to pull price up.
- **Reality:** No upside nodes = No Resistance. This setup allows for a "Full Send" reversal (green candles only) because there are no walls to stop the squeeze [07:42].

### The "Scale-In" Entry

- **Technique:**
  1. Front-run the King Node by entering a Starter Position (25-50%) at ±5 points from the level.
  2. If price dips deeper into the level, scale into Full Size.
  3. Size the position for zero (no hard stop) to survive the liquidity hunt/overshoot [04:45].

## 2. New or Refined Concepts

### Price Delivery Context

- **Concept:** You cannot trade the map in isolation. You must ask: "Where did price come from?"
- **Rule:** If price was delivered from the King Node (it tapped and reversed), the magnet is temporarily "off." Do not bet on a return trip immediately [02:14].

### Node Dissolve vs. Accumulation

- **Signal:** After a bounce, the floor node (e.g., 6600) should shrink (35M → 25M).
- **Warning:** If price bounces but the floor node grows (35M → 60M), this is a **Bearish Divergence**. The bounce is fake, and price will likely retest the lows [09:30].

### The "Abundance Mindset"

- **Psychology:** Stop negotiating for every tick. If you miss a limit order, let it go. There are thousands of trades. Trading from scarcity ("I need this trade to work") leads to desperation and losses [01:04:17].

## 3. Operational Workflow Items

### The "Limit Buy" Negotiation

- **Technique:** Treat the market like a street market. If the ask is $3.50, limit bid at $3.40 or $3.30. Never pay full price.
- **Execution:** Place limit buys on Red Candles (panic selling) to get filled at the bid. Place limit sells on Green Candles (chasers) to sell at the ask [01:02:00].

### Taking Profit (Proactivity)

- **Rule:** Sell calls into massive green halt candles. Do not wait for the candle to turn red or break support. Sell when premiums are inflated by Delta + IV [01:38:03].

### Chart Hygiene

- **Standard:** Glitch trades with Zero Indicators. No RSI, no MACD, no EMAs.
- **Reason:** Indicators are lagging "scapegoats" that prevent you from learning to read raw price action and psychology [01:14:19].

## 4. Rules & Edge Cases

### The "Hot Streak" Kill Switch

- **Rule:** If you find yourself bragging to friends/family about your "Hot Streak," take the week off immediately.
- **Reason:** Overconfidence precedes the market humbling you. Protect your gains by walking away when you feel invincible [28:17].

### No Confirmation Rule

- **Philosophy:** Waiting for confirmation (e.g., a candle close or trendline break) ensures you have a symmetric (1:1) risk/reward.
- **Action:** You must enter before confirmation (at the level) to get the 5:1 or 10:1 risk/reward required for long-term profitability [01:24:48].

### Stop Loss Removal

- **Controversial Rule:** For 0DTE, do not use hard stops. Size the position small enough that you can hold to zero.
- **Logic:** Hard stops get hunted by algorithms. Removing the stop removes the target, provided your position size is manageable [01:33:09].
