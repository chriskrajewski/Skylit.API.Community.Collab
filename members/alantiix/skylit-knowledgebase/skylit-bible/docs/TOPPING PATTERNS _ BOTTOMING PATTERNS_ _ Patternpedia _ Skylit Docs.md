4Though VEX positioning shows how dealers are postured in the days ahead,
the strategy of trading this positioning is relatively straightforward: by playing
node rejections with the knowledge that dealers are postured in an extremely
bearish manner. Keep in mind that maps can shuffle at a moment's notice,
whether that's intraday or for the days ahead.
An invalidation of this bias would simply be the opposite of how it was
