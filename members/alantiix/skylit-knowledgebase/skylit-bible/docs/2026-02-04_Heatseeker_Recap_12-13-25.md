## 1. Concrete Setups & Patterns

### The "Rug Pull" Setup (QQQ Example)

- **Configuration:** A Yellow Pico Node (Positive GEX) stacked directly with a Barney Node (Negative GEX) [05:59].
- **Logic:** The yellow node acts as a temporary ceiling (deflector). If price breaks below the purple (negative) node, it triggers a "sell loop" where dealers must hedge by selling, often leading to a rapid flush.
- **Outcome:** This setup on QQQ signaled a high probability drop to 618 [09:05].

### Trinity Confluence Entry

- **Setup:** All three indices (SPX, SPY, QQQ) hitting resistance simultaneously.

### Double Stacked Yellows (Deflectors)

- **Rule:** Do not bank on price falling through these on the first test. They are highly deflective. Take profits on shorts as price enters this zone [11:24].

## 2. New or Refined Concepts

### Node Velocity (Rate of Growth)

- **Concept:** Monitoring how fast a node grows intraday is more important than its static size.
- **Signal:** A node growing from 30M to 45M (50% increase) rapidly indicates massive dealer obligation accumulating. Even if price bounces at a "Gatekeeper" node above it, this growth suggests the magnet below is becoming stronger [10:54].

### "Proactive" Order Management

- **Technique:** Sell calls into green candles; Sell puts into red candles.
- **Logic:** Waiting for a rejection candle to confirm a top often results in a 20-30% premium loss due to the speed of 0DTE pricing. You must exit into the momentum of the move you predicted [47:29].

### The "Lowball" Limit Snipe

- **Technique:** Instead of market orders, place limit buys significantly below the current bid (e.g., bid is $5.00, place limit at $4.50).
- **Logic:** Capitalizes on panic sellers dumping at market price. You get filled on the wick down, putting you instantly up 10-15% when the candle closes [44:52].

- Zoom into the 3-minute chart for execution [03:42].

### The Lunch Hour Reset

- **Rule:** If you hit 2-3 good trades in the morning, stop trading until after lunch (approx. 1:00 PM EST).
- **Workflow:** Let the board reset. Re-evaluate mainly for a potential "reversal" or "continuation" only after the midday volume lull [17:51].

### Profit Management

- **Scenario:** If a trade is up 100%, take it. Do not hold for 1000% gains based on "what ifs."
- **Reality Check:** Most traders would have sold a 1000% runner during the first 30% pullback anyway. Secure the bag [30:51].
