## 1. Concrete Setups & Patterns

The core strategy revolves around **Node Deflections**—trading the rejection or reversal off major GEX/VEX nodes rather than chasing the momentum into them.

### The "Beach Ball" Reversal (Negative GEX Pit)

- **Setup:** Price drops into a major Negative GEX (purple) node (e.g., SPX 6630/6640 aligned with SPY 660) [01:13:11].
- **Logic:** As puts go "In-The-Money" (ITM), gamblers cash out, forcing dealers to buy back their short hedges, creating a "trampoline effect" [01:13:22].
- **Entry:** Limit buys (lowballing the bid) as price taps the node. Avoid market orders to minimize slippage [35:50].
- **Exit:** Scale out partials at the first "Gatekeeper" (GK) node; hold runners if the 61.8%–79% Fibonacci retracement (Golden Zone) holds [38:36].

### The Upper Node Rejection (Positive GEX King)

- **Setup:** Price approaches a bright Yellow (Positive GEX) King Node (e.g., 6700) in a low-volatility regime [25:01].
- **Logic:** These nodes act as suppressors that dampen volatility, often leading to a rejection [01:10:45].

### Risk/Reward Filter

Only take trades with a minimum **3:1 R/R ratio**. Example provided: 10 points of risk to the upside node vs. 55 points of potential to the downside node (5.5:1) [26:26].

## 2. New or Refined Concepts

- **Node Resuscitation:** Levels that appear to "diminish" on the heatmap as price moves away are often just experiencing delta decay, not necessarily position closing. They can "come back to life" and become magnetic or resistant again if price returns to them [47:51].

- **Pure Gamma Regime:** 0DTE options trade almost exclusively on Gamma because Theta cushion is non-existent. This creates violent feedback loops where dealer hedging dominates "buyer/seller" price discovery [03:55].

- **Vanna/Charm Drift:** Mid-day (post-lunch) often sees a natural drift upward if volatility is stable or compressing. This is due to dealers buying back hedges as Vanna and Charm decay [44:27].

- **The 3-Touch Rule:** Avoid trading the third test of a node. There is a ~66% chance it breaks through on the third attempt [47:16].
