# Futures Clinic Week 2: Chart Literacy & TA Masterclass Analysis

**Source:** [YouTube - Futures Clinic Week 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6ADkLOJqMcM)

This analysis covers the "Futures Clinic Week 2" session hosted by John Wicks, focusing on integrating raw Technical Analysis (TA) on Futures (ES/NQ) with Heatseeker/Skylit concepts. This methodology emphasizes "Chart Hygiene" and utilizing the VIX as a charted instrument.

---

## 1. Concrete Setups & Patterns

### The "Timmy Hunt" (Liquidity Sweep)

- **Setup:** Price pushes slightly beyond a major swing high or low (where retail "Little Timmies" place stops) [15:43].
- **Logic:** Market Makers hunt these stops to create liquidity. Once the stop run is complete (a wick above/below the level), the true reversal begins.
- **Entry:** Enter on the rejection back through the level after the sweep, rather than trying to front-run the level.

### Hourly-to-3-Minute Fractal Entry

- **Setup:** Identify a "Double Top" or "Double Bottom" on the Hourly (1H) chart first [51:24].
- **Trigger:** Once price hits that 1H level, zoom into the 3-minute (3m) chart. Wait for a micro double top/bottom or liquidity hunt on the 3m to execute.
- **Logic:** Prevents trying to catch a falling knife on a macro trend by waiting for micro-structure confirmation.

### Pivot Flips

- **Setup:** Identifying levels where previous resistance clearly became support (or vice versa) on the Hourly chart [20:20].
- **Criteria:** Look for levels that triggered a significant reaction (e.g., a 40-100 point move on ES).

## 2. New or Refined Concepts

### Chart Hygiene (ES/NQ vs. The Trinity)

- **Concept:** Draw Support/Resistance only on Futures (ES/NQ) because they show cleaner overnight highs/lows and have no gaps. Use SPX/SPY/QQQ only for Heatseeker/Gamma levels [07:33].
- **Benefit:** Keeps charts clean. You don't have Gamma lines cluttering your price action charts, allowing for objective "Chart Literacy."

### VIX Technical Analysis

- **Concept:** Charting the VIX like a stock. Drawing support/resistance, double tops, and consolidations on the VIX itself [25:51].
- **Signal:** If VIX hits a charted resistance level (e.g., a previous double top), look for a long entry on SPX/ES.

### Reactionary Levels

- **Definition:** Prioritize levels based on the magnitude of the reaction they caused (e.g., a wick that led to a 200-point rally) rather than how many times they were touched [09:16]. Fresh levels with violent histories are superior to "well-respected" but tapped levels.

### SMT Divergence (Smart Money Tactics)

- **Signal:** When NQ makes a lower low but ES makes a higher low (or vice versa). This divergence often signals the exhaustion of the move and an impending reversal [33:52].

## 3. Operational Workflow Items

### The "Six-Chart" Layout

- Keep six charts open simultaneously: ES, NQ, SPX, SPY, QQQ, and VIX [44:07].
- Use ES/NQ for structure, VIX for sentiment/momentum, and SPX/SPY/QQQ for Gamma confirmation.

### The Hourly Hover Rule

- **Workflow:** Keep your main focus on the Hourly Chart throughout the day. Do not stare at the 3-minute chart unless price is at a key Hourly level [58:10].
- **Why:** Staring at the 3m chart induces overtrading and "hallucinating" setups that are just noise within a larger hourly candle.

### VIX Consolidation Breakout

- **Workflow:** If VIX is consolidating, wait. A breakout from VIX consolidation usually dictates the trend for the day.
- **Example:** VIX breaks up from consolidation → Bearish Trend Day. VIX breaks down → Bullish Trend Day [38:04].

## 4. Rules & Edge Cases

### No Confirmation Waiting

- **Rule:** Do not wait for the Hourly candle to close to enter. Enter as the wick is forming if confluences align (Heatseeker Node + VIX Resistance + ES Structure) [47:13].
- **Logic:** Waiting for the close often means missing the majority of the move in volatile futures/0DTE trading.

### The "Overtrading" Guardrail

- **Rule:** If you find yourself trying to short/long every 3-minute candle, physically switch your view back to the 1-hour chart to reset your bias [59:25].

### News/Event Filters

- **Edge Case:** "Bad charts usually line up with bad news." If the chart looks broken technically, news often follows to accelerate the move. Use the chart as the leading indicator [30:51].
