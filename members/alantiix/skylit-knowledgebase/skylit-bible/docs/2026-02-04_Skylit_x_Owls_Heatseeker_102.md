# Skylit x Owls (Heatseeker 102) Analysis

**Source:** [YouTube - Skylit x Owls 102](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XSybHIpQ078)

This analysis covers the "Heatseeker 102" session by Glitch, which focuses on application and trade selection. Unlike the 101 session (theory), this session emphasizes how to scan for "Asymmetric Risk/Reward" setups and filter out bad trades.

---

## 1. Concrete Setups & Patterns

### The "Slingshot" Setup (LLY Example)

- **Configuration:** Price dips into a major Floor Node (e.g., 750) with a massive gap to the next upside King Node (e.g., 800) [50:02].
- **Logic:** A clean range with no intermediate "junk" nodes creates a high-velocity move.
- **Execution:** Buy the dip into the floor (750).
- **Outcome:** Zero drawdown trade that captured a ~30 point move.

### The "Liquidity Hunt" Entry (Purple Nodes)

- **Concept:** Purple (Negative GEX) nodes represent high volatility ("fuel on fire"). They often get "hunted" or undercut before the reversal.
- **Execution:** Do not buy at market. Set lowball limit buys significantly below the bid as price approaches the purple node to catch the wick [12:03].

### The "Asymmetric" Filter (HOOD Example)

- **Criteria:** Only take trades where the distance to the Target Node is at least 3x the distance to the Stop/Floor Node (3:1 R/R).
- **Example:** HOOD bouncing off 115 (Floor) with a target of 125 (King Node).
- **Avoid:** "Symmetric" setups where price is stuck in the middle (e.g., IONQ with 3 pts upside vs 7 pts downside) [23:24].

## 2. New or Refined Concepts

### VEX for Swings (Visualizing the Staircase)

- **Concept:** For multi-week swing trades, ignore GEX (Gamma) and look at VEX (Volatility Exposure).
- **Pattern:** Look for a "Stair Step Up and to the Right" pattern on the VEX map (e.g., LULU). This indicates dealers are positioned for a sustained grind higher over weeks [24:44].

### Hedge Nodes vs. Targets (OpEx Warning)

- **Concept:** During Quad Witching/OpEx (3rd Friday of the month), massive deep OTM nodes (e.g., QQQ 575 when price is 592) are often Hedge Nodes, not price targets.
- **Rule:** Take OpEx nodes with a "grain of salt." They often expire worthless or wash out [13:47].

### The "Low Liquidity" Void

- **Limitation:** Heatseeker is ineffective on tickers where GEX nodes are < 4 digits (e.g., Wingstop with 99k notional).
- **Rule:** If the node value is not in the millions (or high hundreds of thousands), the magnet is too weak to rely on [01:13:04].

## 3. Operational Workflow Items

### The "Index Chop" Pivot

- **Workflow:** If SPY and QQQ are divergent (one bullish, one bearish) or "muted," stop trading indices.
- **Action:** Immediately pivot to Single Tickers (Stocks). When the broad market chops, relative strength/weakness in individual names (like LLY or HOOD) becomes cleaner and more profitable [10:53].

### Scanning Routine

- **Technique:** Use the arrow keys to rapidly scroll through 300+ tickers.
- **Visual Filter:** Stop only when you see a gray box (current price) significantly far away from a bright yellow/purple bar (King Node). If price is near the bar, skip it [21:25].

### Gatekeeper Management

- **Rule:** Yellow nodes act as suppressors. If you are long and price hits a Yellow Node (e.g., Meta at 780), trim the majority. Do not hold for the breakout unless the node dissolves [59:04].

## 4. Rules & Edge Cases

### The "Too Good to Be True" Rule

- **Scenario:** A massive downside target appears that defies fundamentals (e.g., Oracle pointing to 230 after a good earnings beat).
- **Action:** Treat this as a Lotto/Hedge only (0.1% risk). Do not full-port based on the map alone if it contradicts the fundamental/chart reality [34:47].

### All-Time Highs (ATH) Caution

- **Rule:** Avoid trading tickers at ATH (e.g., T2, Tesla). The lack of overhead structure makes "False Breakouts" highly probable. Better to trade reversals or bounces from structure [01:01:34].
