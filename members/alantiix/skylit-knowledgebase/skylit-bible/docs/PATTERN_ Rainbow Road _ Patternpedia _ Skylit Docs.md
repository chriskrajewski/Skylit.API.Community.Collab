1PATTERN: Rainbow Road
This heatmap is characterized by having multiple prominent nodes of positive
and negative values, in many cases bearing a resemblance to a rainbow.
Another distinct feature of this heatmap is the lack of a clear range for price
to trade within, heralding choppy and erratic price action.
It is highly advisable to avoid trading when positioning is vague. Instead, wait
for a higher probability setup to form.
