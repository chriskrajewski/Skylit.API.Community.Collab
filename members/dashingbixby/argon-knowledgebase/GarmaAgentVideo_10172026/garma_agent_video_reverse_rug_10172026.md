# Reverse Rug Setup (Bullish)

## Structure
- **Negative gamma node** on top.
- **Positive gamma node** below.
- Spot price is **above the positive gamma node**.
Visual: purple node above, yellow node below, price sitting on the positive node.

## Behavior
- Positive gamma node below = deflects price back up.
- Negative gamma node above = fuels the deflection back up (dealers hedge with the move once it accelerates).
This is **"support with pro-cyclical momentum."** Price dips, holds, then reverses with momentum behind the bounce.

## Mental Model
Instead of rejection downward, you get hold + bounce.
The floor doesn't just stop price — it launches price.

## Why It Works
- The positive gamma below provides hedging support (dealers buy dips).
- Once price bounces into the negative gamma zone above, dealer hedging flips into fuel (dealers buy into the rally).
- What starts as a normal floor bounce gets amplified into an expansion higher.

## How to Trade It
- **Wait for the direct tap of the positive gamma floor.** That's the entry, not before.
- Confirm that the floor is holding (no clean break + hold below).
- Target the next node up or play delivery through an upside air pocket.
- **Stop:** Break and hold one node below the positive gamma floor.

## Confluence Multipliers
The reverse rug is strongest when:
- Trinity aligns (2/3 or 3/3 indices support upside).
- Chart shows support at the same area (higher timeframe, double bottom, prior swing low).
- Negative gamma above has rapid accumulation (Velocity Mode building upward).
- Downside floors have rolled up to this strike.

## Specific Power Setup: Double Bottom + Reverse Rug
When a chart double bottom aligns with a reverse rug on the heatmap, the setup is high conviction. The double bottom provides chart-side evidence that buyers defended the level twice, and the reverse rug provides the dealer-positioning evidence that the bounce will accelerate.
