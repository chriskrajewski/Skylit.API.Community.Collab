## Canonical Naming Convention

Where Skylit sources use multiple terms for the same concept, this knowledge base standardizes on one **Canonical Term** and lists synonyms in parentheses:
- **Reverse Rug Setup** *(Synonym: Inverse Rug Setup)*[cite: 1]
- **Positive Gamma Node** *(Slang: Pika Node, Yellow Node)*[cite: 1]
- **Negative Gamma Node** *(Slang: Barney Node, Purple Node)*[cite: 1]
- **Beach Ball Setup** *(Synonym: Overshoot Setup)*[cite: 1]
- **Delivered Node** *(Subsequent decay stage: Decaying Node / Spent Node)*[cite: 1]

## Alphabetical Glossary

### A
- **A+ Setup Framework:** The mandatory four-part qualification standard for taking a trade in Skylit: (1) Chart structure comes first, (2) Heatseeker positioning provides confluence, (3) Asymmetric risk-to-reward of at least 3:1, and (4) Execution occurs on reaction/deflection at the node rather than anticipation *(Summer Bootcamp adds a 4th gate: mapped liquidity sweep)* `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Ch. 12, Flowseeker Ch. 10]`[cite: 1]. *See `charts_first_workflow.md`.*
- **Absolute Value Rule:** The foundational Heatseeker rule that the magnitude (dollar size) of a node determines its gravitational pull and hedging influence, overriding its sign or color (`+GEX` yellow vs. `-GEX` purple) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 2, Sec. 5; Docs Core Concepts]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Acceptance (Price Acceptance):** Price breaking beyond a structural level or node and holding there across subsequent candles (rather than wicking through and snapping back), which invalidates a deflection thesis `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Sec. 3 & 10; Flowseeker Ch. 7, 9, 10]`[cite: 1]. *See `entries_stops_targets_and_rr.md`.*
- **Aegis:** Skylit's integrated sidebar/panel system within Atlas that surfaces contextual modules such as the 30-session Tempest Sigma strip `[DOCTRINE: Docs Tempest Field Guide]`. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*
- **Air Pocket:** A strike zone on the Heatseeker map with low or weak dealer GEX exposure between larger structural nodes, acting as a low-friction "hallway" or pathway through which price travels rapidly `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 3, Sec. 8; Ch. 8, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_spatio_temporal_relationships.md`.*
- **Ask Side:** Options prints that execute just below, at, or above the national best ask price, indicating an urgent buyer paying up to fill immediately `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 2, Sec. 5]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Asymmetric Risk-to-Reward:** A trade structure where potential reward to the next structural target is a multiple of the distance to the invalidation stop loss—standardized at a minimum of **3:1** in Skylit doctrine `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 3, Sec. 6; Ch. 6, Sec. 3 & 9]`[cite: 1]. *See `entries_stops_targets_and_rr.md`.*
- **Atlas:** Skylit's native charting platform (powered by TradingView Lightweight Charts™) that overlays Heatseeker GEX/VEX nodes as dynamic Orbs, Dark Pool block levels as horizontal dashed lines, and options flow as a synchronized subgraph pane `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Course; Docs Atlas Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*

### B
- **Barney Node:** Community slang for a **Negative Gamma (`-GEX`)** node, which renders as purple in the default Heatseeker palette and amplifies volatility via pro-cyclical dealer hedging `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 2, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Beach Ball (Overshoot Setup):** A pattern where price punches through a major node (most frequently a negative gamma / Barney node) like a beach ball pushed underwater, trapping momentum chasers before stalling and snapping back strongly in the opposite direction `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 5, Sec. 3.IV]`[cite: 1]. *See `setup_catalog_beach_ball_and_pika_cloud.md`.*
- **Bid Side:** Options prints that execute below, at, or just above the national best bid price, indicating an urgent seller hitting the bid to fill immediately `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 2, Sec. 5]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Bull vs. Bear Tab:** The third tab on a Talon read card that lists bullish and bearish arguments with nearest levels first (top 3 upfront) and indicates whether a VEX pull is armed `[DOCTRINE: Changelog Oct 10 & Oct 13, 2026]`. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*

### C
- **CANCELLED Badge:** A badge and strikethrough applied to a row in Flowseeker's Live Feed indicating that an options exchange voided the trade after execution; cancelled prints must be ignored `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 2, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Ceiling:** The largest dealer exposure node located **above** current spot price, acting as a structural resistance zone where dealer hedging opposes upward movement `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 3, Sec. 5]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Charts First Doctrine:** Skylit's foundational rule that a trader must establish a directional or structural thesis from raw price action (support, resistance, trend, range extremes) *before* looking at Heatseeker or Flowseeker `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 1, Sec. 1 & 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `charts_first_workflow.md`.*
- **Cheap Fill:** Entering a deflection trade directly at a major node as emotional retail traders panic-exit at the extreme, rather than chasing momentum after the reversal has already begun `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `entries_stops_targets_and_rr.md`.*
- **Cividis:** The recommended color-blind-safe palette among Heatseeker's seven color maps `[DOCTRINE: Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*
- **Confluence:** The alignment of multiple independent layers of evidence around the same price zone—specifically Chart Structure + Heatseeker Node + Trinity Cross-Index Agreement (+ optional Dark Pool level and Flowseeker activity) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Sec. 2; Ch. 10; Flowseeker Ch. 6 & 7]`[cite: 1]. *See `charts_first_workflow.md`.*
- **Connected Springs Analogy:** The principle from Chapter 10 that `SPX`, `SPY`, and `QQQ` are three interconnected expressions of a single market-maker exposure system; one index cannot cleanly bounce or break down if the others strongly oppose the move `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 10, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `trinity_and_cross_index.md`.*
- **Contract Drilldown:** A detailed Flowseeker view for a single options contract showing `Contract Flow` (volume by bid/mid/ask), `Net Premium`, `Strike Distribution`, `Underlying (Vol / $)`, `Vol/OI History`, and `Flow Orders` `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 6, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Contract Flow Chart:** A bar chart inside Flowseeker's Contract Drilldown showing contract volume broken down by bid, mid, and ask over time (not a candlestick chart of option price) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 6, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Contract Lookup:** A Flowseeker view providing a single-ticker options overview `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 6, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Current Expiration (`Front`):** The nearest/same-day contract expiration column on Heatseeker or Atlas, used primarily by 0DTE and intraday scalpers `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Sec. 3, 5, 9; Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `gex_vs_vex_and_expirations.md`.*

### D
- **Dark Feed:** The scrolling table in Flowseeker (and Live Feed's Dark pool tape block in Talon) that displays live and historical off-exchange equity block trades, defaulting to a **$1,000,000 notional minimum** `[DOCTRINE: Academy Dark Pool Course, Sec. 3; Changelog Oct 14, 2026]`[cite: 1]. *See `dark_pool.md`.*
- **Dark Pool Overlay:** An Atlas chart setting that plots the `Top N` (`1`, `2`, `3`, or `5`) largest off-exchange equity prints over a selected `Lookback` (`30`, `45`, `90`, or `180` days) as horizontal dashed lines labeled with notional value and date (e.g., `DP $2.2B · 5/15`) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Dark Pool Course, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `dark_pool.md`.*
- **Dark Pool Print:** An off-exchange equity block trade reported after execution with six fields (Date/Time, Ticker, Price, Size in shares, Notional in dollars, GICS Sector) and **zero directional side** `[DOCTRINE: Academy Dark Pool Course, Sec. 1 & 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `dark_pool.md`.*
- **Decaying Node / Spent Node:** The fourth and final stage of the node lifecycle, where a node's exposure shrinks over time or its liquidity has been exhausted by repeated taps, rendering it low-probability for further deflection `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 9, Sec. 2; Talon Prompt Guide, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Decoy Node (Speculative Node):** A large node located far out-of-the-money that lacks intermediate pathway structure or growth and acts as a trap/protection rather than a realistic intraday magnet `[DOCTRINE: Docs Patternpedia: Case Study Speculative and Decoy Nodes]`. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Deflection:** A price bounce off a Floor or rejection off a Ceiling/King Node driven by dealer hedge rebalancing and retail panic liquidation at the strike `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Sec. 3 & 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `entries_stops_targets_and_rr.md`.*
- **Deflection Zone:** The price tolerance band around a node strike within which a valid direct-tap deflection and trade entry can occur: **$\pm\$0.50$ on SPY and QQQ**, and **$\pm\$5.00$ on SPX** *(Knowledge Hub 0DTE SPX guide also cites a **5–10 point** SPX margin)* `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Sec. 3; Learn: Best GEX Tools for 0DTE SPX]`[cite: 1]. *See `entries_stops_targets_and_rr.md`.*
- **Delivered Node:** The third stage of the node lifecycle: a node that price has tapped, reacted from, and moved away from; its primary liquidity has been used and its future stopping power is reduced `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 9, Sec. 2 & 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Derived Orbs (Derived Exposure):** GEX/VEX node levels on Atlas or Heatseeker that are mathematically mapped onto an instrument without its own primary options chain (or cross-referenced between related chains): `ES` borrows from `SPXW/SPY`, `NQ` borrows from `QQQ`, and `SPY` and `SPXW` cross-reference `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Course, Sec. 8; Docs Atlas Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `trinity_and_cross_index.md`.*
- **Direct Tap:** Price reaching and touching a node's strike (within its Deflection Zone) for the first time in a move, serving as the primary entry trigger in Skylit execution doctrine `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `entries_stops_targets_and_rr.md`.*
- **Divergence (Cross-Index Divergence):** A condition where `SPX`, `SPY`, and `QQQ` show conflicting directional biases or structural positioning (e.g., SPX holding a King Node floor while SPY/QQQ point lower); treated as a warning to reduce size or stand down `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 10, Sec. 4; Flowseeker Ch. 7, Sec. 5]`[cite: 1]. *See `trinity_and_cross_index.md`.*
- **Drives Away:** Market Maker behavior common **early in the trading session** where price reaches the King Node too early to be pinned all day, prompting dealers to push/drive price away from the King Node `[DOCTRINE: Docs Core Concepts]`. *See `session_timing.md`.*
- **DTE (Days to Expiration):** The number of calendar/trading days until an options contract expires, grouped in Flowseeker into three interpretive buckets: `0–7 DTE` (this week/event), `8–30 DTE` (classic positioning window), and `31+ DTE` (longer horizon/hedging common) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 2, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*

### E
- **Edge / Proven Edge:** Performance ranking metrics displayed on Nexus leaderboards, podium cards, and Live Stages profile cards (replacing "Cred" and primary "Avg Return" in October 2026) `[DOCTRINE: Changelog Oct 13, 2026]`. *See `risk_sizing_and_discipline.md`.*
- **Entry Column:** On Talon's Swing and Position heatmaps, the nearest expiration column, which is always pinned and labeled as `"Entry"` so swing traders can time intraday entries using front-expiry GEX while targeting further-dated VEX `[DOCTRINE: Changelog Oct 13, 2026]`. *See `gex_vs_vex_and_expirations.md`.*
- **Expected Move:** A Tempest volatility reading that translates implied volatility into the dollar/percentage price range options are pricing over a given horizon, used as a yardstick for targets, stops, and strike selection `[DOCTRINE: Docs Tempest Field Guide; Learn: Tempest Accuracy]`. *See `tempest_volatility.md`.*

### F
- **FIR:** A market-wide flow metric displayed in the Flowseeker Live Feed top summary bar alongside directional sentiment, net premium, calls/puts, put/call ratio, and RVOL `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 5, Sec. 5]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md` and `open_questions.md`.*
- **Floor:** The largest dealer exposure node located **below** current spot price, acting as a structural support zone where dealer hedging slows or reverses downward movement `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 3, Sec. 5]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Flow Compass:** A Flowseeker view where each item is a ranked summary card highlighting what stands out across the market `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 6, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Flow Pane (Atlas Flow Subgraph):** A timeline-synced subgraph pane beneath an Atlas price chart where vertical bars plot **option premium in dollars** (calls above zero, puts below zero, plus an optional call-minus-put Net line), filtered by 7 pill groups `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 8, Sec. 2 & 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Flow Scanner:** A Flowseeker view where each row represents **one contract rolled up across the entire trading day** (showing daily volume, OI, OI change, total premium, IV, and bullish/bearish split) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 6, Sec. 2 & 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Flow Score:** A directional classification score assigned to options prints in Flowseeker ranging from **$-100$ (strongly bearish) to $+100$ (strongly bullish)** (with near $0$ = unclear), calculated from side, sweeps, moneyness, DTE, premium, and implied volatility. It sorts attention and is **not** a win probability `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 5, Sec. 2 & 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Flow Tracker:** A Flowseeker tool with two modes—`Tracked Flow` (saved individual prints marked against current mid vs. original fill price) and `Tracked Contracts` (saved contracts showing current mid and spot) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 6, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **FlowBonus:** Skylit's "how unusual" metric quoted by Flow AI; on the Live Feed, the `Premium vs Ticker History` filter (20-day percentile rank) and `Vol/OI` answer the same question `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 5, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Flowseeker:** Skylit's options flow and dark pool intelligence suite (comprising Live Feed, Flow Scanner, Flow Compass, Contract Lookup, Contract Drilldown, Flow Tracker, and Dark Feed) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 1–10; Docs Flowseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Focus Preset:** A Heatseeker display preset (under the Sliders menu) that displays node values as a percentage share of the King Node (`% King`, where King = `100%`) and hides or dims small nodes `[DOCTRINE: Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*
- **Fresh Level / Fresh Node:** A chart support/resistance zone or Heatseeker node that has **not yet been tapped/tested** during the relevant session/horizon; carries full resting liquidity and an **~80% reaction probability** on first touch `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 1, Sec. 4; Ch. 6, Sec. 6; Ch. 9, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*

### G
- **Gamma Flip:** A price-action event where underlying price breaks through and holds above (or below) a key node, potentially converting a former resistance node into a floor (distinct from a **Rolling Floor**, which is a dealer positioning shift before price breaks) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 7, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_spatio_temporal_relationships.md`.*
- **Gamma Regime:** The overarching dealer hedging environment—either **Positive Gamma (`+GEX`)** (stabilizing, mean-reverting, volatility-suppressing) or **Negative Gamma (`-GEX`)** (expanding, fast, volatility-amplifying)—which governs *how* price moves between levels rather than direction `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 2, Sec. 3; Ch. 4, Sec. 1 & 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Gatekeeper Node:** An intermediary node sitting between spot/floor and a larger structural node (such as the King Node) that acts like a checkpoint or "bouncer at a nightclub door," regulating whether price can transition into the next exposure zone `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 3, Sec. 7; Docs Core Concepts]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md` and `setup_catalog_fade_gatekeeper_and_vex_swing.md`.*
- **GEX (Net Gamma Exposure):** Modeled dealer sensitivity to underlying price movement across strikes; dominant in short-term/0DTE trading and used for intraday support, resistance, and deflection entries `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Sec. 10; Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `gex_vs_vex_and_expirations.md`.*

### H
- **Heatseeker™:** Skylit's flagship dealer positioning heatmap that models net GEX and VEX across strikes and expirations `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 1–13; Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Hedge Node:** A large node typically located far out-of-the-money (often appearing ahead of macro events like FOMC, CPI, JOLTS, NFP, or earnings) that remains static or slowly unwinds/decays over time; represents portfolio insurance/protection rather than active directional intent `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 9, Sec. 5; Docs Core Concepts]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Hierarchy of Influence:** The four-tier priority order used when patterns or nodes conflict: (1) **Node Magnitude**, (2) **Gamma Regime**, (3) **Pattern Structure**, and (4) **Cross-Index Alignment** `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 5, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_advanced_mechanics_and_agent_guardrails.md`.*
- **Hindsight Trap:** The cognitive bias of looking back at a chart and remembering flow spikes that preceded large price moves while forgetting identical flow spikes that occurred in midpoints and produced nothing `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 8, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*

### I
- **Initial Balance (IB):** An Atlas indicator (introduced as a standalone indicator in Oct 2026) that plots the high, low, midpoint line, Fibonacci retracement lines ("golden zone"), and optional extension ladder for the first `15`, `30`, or `60` minutes of the New York, London, or Asia session `[DOCTRINE: Changelog Oct 13, 2026]`. *See `chart_structure_fundamentals.md`.*
- **IV Rank:** A Tempest reading that measures where a stock's current implied volatility (`SVX`) sits relative to its own past-year range `[DOCTRINE: Docs Tempest Field Guide; Learn: Tempest Accuracy]`. *See `tempest_volatility.md`.*

### K
- **Killzones:** An Atlas indicator (replacing Session Zones in Oct 2026 on charts $\le 30\text{m}$) that draws session boxes with high and low lines for `ASIA`, `LNDN`, `NYAM`, and `NYPM` `[DOCTRINE: Changelog Oct 13, 2026]`. *See `chart_structure_fundamentals.md` and `session_timing.md`.*
- **King Node (`KING`):** The strike with the **single largest absolute exposure value** on a Heatseeker map (marked with a star on the heatmap and configurable via `King Opacity` on Atlas); acts as the primary gravitational magnet and late-day pinning target `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 3, Sec. 4; Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*

### L
- **Liquidity Vacuum:** A wide, extended region of sparse or weak dealer exposure across multiple strikes lacking floors or ceilings; acts as a "freeway" where price can enter free-fall or vertical expansion `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 8, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_spatio_temporal_relationships.md`.*
- **Live Feed:** Flowseeker's real-time scrolling options tape where **one row equals one executed options print** (or grouped sweep) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 1, Sec. 2; Ch. 6, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*

### M
- **Midpoint:** The equilibrium zone roughly halfway between a major Floor and Ceiling (or Range High and Range Low) where dealer hedging pressure is weakest, direction is least clear, and risk-to-reward is at best 1:1; strictly off-limits for trade entries ("do not diddle in the middle") `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 1, Sec. 4; Ch. 3, Sec. 6; Ch. 6, Sec. 7; Docs Ten Commandments]`[cite: 1]. *See `no_trade_conditions.md`.*
- **Moneyness:** The percentage distance of an option's strike from spot price at execution; in Flowseeker, **positive (`+%`) = out of the money (OTM)**, **negative (`-%`) = in the money (ITM)**, and **near `0%` = at the money (ATM)** `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 2, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Multi-Leg Trade:** An options strategy where two or more contracts are traded simultaneously as a single package (such as a vertical spread or straddle) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 3, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*

### N
- **Negative Gamma (`-GEX`):** Dealer short-gamma positioning (colored purple / "Barney" in default Heatseeker) where dealers must hedge *with* the underlying move (buying as price rises, selling as price falls), amplifying volatility and causing node overshoots `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 2, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Nexus:** Skylit's paper-trading, journaling, leaderboard (`Rank` and `Season` boards), and trade-review platform for options, stocks, and futures (with a $100,000 paper wallet and API/Agent account support) `[DOCTRINE: Docs Nexus Field Guide; Changelog Oct 13–14, 2026]`. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*
- **Node:** A cell on the Heatseeker heatmap (or Orb on Atlas) representing net modeled dealer exposure (GEX or VEX) at a specific strike price and expiration `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 2 & 3; Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Notional:** In the Dark Feed, the total dollar value of an off-exchange equity block trade, calculated as $\text{Share Price} \times \text{Shares (Size)}$ `[DOCTRINE: Academy Dark Pool Course, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `dark_pool.md`.*

### O
- **Open Interest (OI):** The total number of options contracts that remain open and unclosed as of the last OCC clearing cycle (updated overnight, meaning intraday OI reflects **yesterday's close**) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 4, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Orbs (Classic & V2):** The circular visual overlays on Atlas charts that represent Heatseeker nodes at their respective strike prices, expanding and contracting in real time as dealer exposure changes `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Course, Sec. 1, 6, 7]`[cite: 1]. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*

### P
- **Patternpedia:** Skylit's encyclopedia of recurring Heatseeker structures, case studies, and the Ten Commandments, authored by Warren and John Wicks `[DOCTRINE: Docs Patternpedia]`. *See `setup_catalog_rug_and_reverse_rug.md` et al.*
- **Pika Cloud:** A dense cluster of multiple **Positive Gamma (`+GEX` / yellow)** nodes stacked across adjacent strikes that acts as a "gravity well" and friction zone, slowing price action, halting trends, and pinning price `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 5, Sec. 3.III]`[cite: 1]. *See `setup_catalog_beach_ball_and_pika_cloud.md`.*
- **Pika Node:** Community slang for a **Positive Gamma (`+GEX`)** node, which renders as yellow in the default Heatseeker palette and dampens volatility `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 2, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Pin Job:** Market Maker behavior common **near the end of the trading day** where price is held in a tight range around the King Node into the NYSE close `[DOCTRINE: Docs Core Concepts; Academy Ch. 3, Sec. 4]`[cite: 1]. *See `session_timing.md`.*
- **Playbook:** A standardized 7-field form (`Question`, `Location`, `Map`, `Flow`, `Target`, `Weakens If`, `Decision`) used to organize and test a trading hypothesis before execution, where flow is never the trigger `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 10, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `charts_first_workflow.md` and `risk_sizing_and_discipline.md`.*
- **Positive Gamma (`+GEX`):** Dealer long-gamma positioning (colored yellow / "Pika" in default Heatseeker) where dealers hedge *against* the underlying move (buying dips, selling rips), suppressing volatility and promoting mean reversion `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 2, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Premium:** The total dollar value of an options print, calculated for standard equity options as $\text{Fill Price} \times \text{Contracts (Size)} \times 100$ `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 2, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Premium vs. Ticker History:** A Flowseeker filter that ranks a print's dollar premium against that specific ticker's own options prints over the trailing **20 trading days** `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 5, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Print:** A single recorded trade execution on the options tape (Flowseeker) or off-exchange equity tape (Dark Feed) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 1, Sec. 2; Dark Pool Course, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*

### R
- **Rainbow Road:** A chaotic Heatseeker map characterized by multiple prominent positive and negative nodes spread across a wide range with no dominant King Node and no defined Floor or Ceiling; strictly a **no-trade condition** `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 5, Sec. 3.VI; Docs Patternpedia: Rainbow Road]`[cite: 1]. *See `no_trade_conditions.md`.*
- **Rate of Change (Velocity):** The speed at which dealer exposure at a node is growing (accumulating) or shrinking (unwinding/melting) over a selected time interval (`1m`, `5m`, `10m`, `15m`, `1h`, `4h`, `1d`), representing dealer urgency ("[Fuel]") `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 8, Sec. 5 & 6; Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_spatio_temporal_relationships.md`.*
- **Real Node:** A node (near or far from spot) that **grows in magnitude over time**, signaling genuine positioning intent and validating the strike as a legitimate target if supported by a structural pathway `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 9, Sec. 5]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Reshuffle (Map Reshuffle):** A dynamic shift in the Heatseeker map where nodes grow, shrink, or migrate to new strikes due to new positions opening, old positions closing, dealer re-hedging, price movement, or time decay `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Sec. 5; Flowseeker Ch. 7, Sec. 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_spatio_temporal_relationships.md`.*
- **Reverse Rug Setup (Inverse Rug Setup):** A bullish composite node pattern where a **Negative Gamma (`-GEX` purple)** node sits stacked directly **above** a **Positive Gamma (`+GEX` yellow)** floor node, with spot price approaching from above; the positive node deflects price upward and the negative node above fuels pro-cyclical upward acceleration `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 5, Sec. 3.II; Ch. 10, Sec. 12]`[cite: 1]. *See `setup_catalog_rug_and_reverse_rug.md`.*
- **Rolling Ceiling Down:** A bearish positioning shift where upside nodes/targets shrink in value and the structural ceiling moves to a **lower strike**, compressing upside room `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 4, Sec. 4; Ch. 7, Sec. 1]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_spatio_temporal_relationships.md`.*
- **Rolling Floor Up:** A bullish positioning shift where downside nodes shrink in value and the structural floor moves to a **higher strike**, lifting the "trap door" underneath price and reducing downside distance `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 4, Sec. 4; Ch. 7, Sec. 1]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_spatio_temporal_relationships.md`.*
- **Rug Setup (Rug Pull Setup):** A bearish composite node pattern where a **Positive Gamma (`+GEX` yellow)** ceiling node is stacked directly **above** one or more **Negative Gamma (`-GEX` purple)** nodes, with spot price below the positive node; price rejects off the positive ceiling (or the yellow node unwinds) and accelerates rapidly downward through the negative gamma zone `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 5, Sec. 3.I; Docs Patternpedia: Rug Setup]`[cite: 1]. *See `setup_catalog_rug_and_reverse_rug.md`.*

### S
- **Scroll as Replay:** An Orbs V2 setting on Atlas that restricts chart orbs to only the historical dealer positioning known at the exact bar/timestamp being viewed when scrolling backward `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Course, Sec. 7; Docs Atlas Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*
- **Sigma Strip:** A Tempest view (showing 60 sessions in Tempest or 30 sessions in the Atlas Aegis tab) that plots each daily move in standard-deviation (`Sigma`) units against the stock's own implied move `[DOCTRINE: Docs Tempest Field Guide]`. *See `tempest_volatility.md`.*
- **Size:** In Flowseeker options views, the **number of option contracts** in a print; in the Dark Feed, the **number of equity shares** in a block trade `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 2, Sec. 3; Ch. 6, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Spot at Execution:** The underlying asset price at the exact moment an options print filled; strikes and moneyness must always be measured against Spot at Execution rather than current spot price `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 2, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **Stairstepping:** A multi-expiration structure where dominant nodes stack at progressively higher (or lower) strikes week-over-week across expiration columns, indicating structural trend formation `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 9, Sec. 8]`[cite: 1]. *See `gex_vs_vex_and_expirations.md` and `setup_catalog_fade_gatekeeper_and_vex_swing.md`.*
- **Summary Bar:** The top metrics bar in Flowseeker's Live Feed showing market-wide sentiment, net premium, FIR, calls/puts, P/C ratio, and RVOL; it reflects the **entire market** and never changes when you filter your tab `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 5, Sec. 5]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*
- **SVX (Skylit Volatility Index / Implied Volatility):** Tempest's standardized implied volatility reading for a stock or index across horizons (e.g., `SVX30`, `Term 9-30`) `[DOCTRINE: Docs Tempest Field Guide; Learn: Tempest Accuracy]`. *See `tempest_volatility.md`.*
- **Sweep:** A single urgent options order split across multiple exchanges that prints on the **same contract within 1 second**; grouped by Flowseeker into one row showing total contracts and premium `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 3, Sec. 2 & 3]`[cite: 1]. *See `flowseeker_options_flow.md`.*

### T
- **Talon:** Two connected Skylit intelligence products: (1) **Talon Chat**, the context-aware AI analyst inside the terminal that reads the current page, heatmap, flow, and docs, and (2) **Talon Dashboard / Scans**, the multi-board workspace (`Scalp`, `Swing`, `Prep`, `Flow`) and setup scanner `[DOCTRINE: Academy Talon Prompt Guide; Docs Talon Field Guide; Changelog Oct 14, 2026]`[cite: 1]. *See `platform_reference_atlas_heatseeker_talon.md`.*
- **Tap Probability Rule:** The empirical/heuristic degradation schedule for node reactions in Chapter 6: **1st Tap = ~80% reaction probability**, **2nd Tap = ~66% reaction probability**, and **3rd Tap = ~33% reaction probability** `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Sec. 6; Ch. 13, Q11]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md` and `entries_stops_targets_and_rr.md`.*
- **Tempest:** Skylit's implied and realized volatility platform (`Radar`, `Market`, `SVX`, `Expected Move`, `IV Rank`, `Term 9-30`, `Curve`, `Skew`, `Sigma`) `[DOCTRINE: Docs Tempest Field Guide]`. *See `tempest_volatility.md`.*
- **Ten Commandments of Using Heatseeker:** Ten core risk-management and discipline rules published in Patternpedia by Warren and John Wicks `[DOCTRINE: Docs Patternpedia: Ten Commandments]`. *See `risk_sizing_and_discipline.md`.*
- **Tested Node:** The second stage of the node lifecycle, where price has interacted with a node once and reacted (~66% reaction probability on the next tap) `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 6, Sec. 6; Ch. 9, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_core_attributes.md`.*
- **Trinity (Trinity Mode):** Skylit's side-by-side multi-index heatmap view (defaulting to **`SPX`/`SPXW`, `SPY`, and `QQQ`**, and supporting up to 10 heatmaps in Atlas sidecar) used to verify system-wide dealer alignment `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 10, Sec. 5; Docs Atlas Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `trinity_and_cross_index.md`.*

### V
- **Velocity Mode:** A Heatseeker toggle (the lightning icon) that switches node display to **percentage rate of change (`% change`)** over a chosen lookback (`1m`, `5m`, `10m`, `15m`, `1h`, `4h`, `1d`) to highlight rapid dealer accumulation ("building") or unwinding ("melting") `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 4, Sec. 3; Ch. 8, Sec. 6; Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `heatseeker_node_spatio_temporal_relationships.md`.*
- **VEX (Net Vanna Exposure):** Modeled dealer sensitivity to changes in implied volatility and time ($\partial \Delta / \partial \sigma$); used for multi-day/swing directional bias (`~5 days and out`), macro price targets, and days when volatility spikes `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Sec. 10; Ch. 9, Sec. 7; Docs Heatseeker Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `gex_vs_vex_and_expirations.md`.*

### W
- **Weakens If (Invalidation):** The mandatory, concrete condition written into every Playbook and read (e.g., "price accepts below 578", "floor node shrinks", "OI falls back") that disproves or weakens a trade hypothesis `[DOCTRINE: Academy Flowseeker Ch. 6, Sec. 5; Ch. 7, Sec. 5; Ch. 10, Sec. 2]`[cite: 1]. *See `charts_first_workflow.md`.*
- **Whipsaw Setup:** A market environment where floor/ceiling ranges exist, but `SPX`, `SPY`, and `QQQ` show conflicting directional biases (e.g., SPX bullish, QQQ bearish, SPY pinned), producing fake breakouts and violent reversals; traders must either stand down or fade only the extreme outer edges `[DOCTRINE: Academy Ch. 4, Sec. 3; Ch. 5, Sec. 3.V]`[cite: 1]. *See `no_trade_conditions.md`.*
- **Wiggle ("The Wiggle"):** The expected minute-to-minute drift of **Derived Orbs** on `ES`, `NQ`, `SPY`, or `SPXW` as Atlas dynamically recalculates borrowed strike levels to stay synced with live price basis changes between the source and target instruments `[DOCTRINE: Academy Atlas Course, Sec. 8; Docs Atlas Field Guide]`[cite: 1]. *See `trinity_and_cross_index.md`.*
